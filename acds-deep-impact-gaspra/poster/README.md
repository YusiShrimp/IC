# Poster

Your group's poster goes in this directory. See the **Poster Presentation** section of
`ProjectDescription.ipynb`, and the project briefing from Monday, for what the poster
should contain and how it will be assessed.

**Deadline: Friday 20th November, 16:30 UTC.** Commit and push your final poster before then.

## What's here

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `Poster_Template.pptx` | An optional A0 portrait PowerPoint template, with both logos already in place |
| `Imperial_ALA_logo_blue.png` | Imperial Ada Lovelace Academy logo (Imperial Blue, transparent background) |
| `RSM175-logo-black.png` | RSM 175 logo, for light backgrounds |
| `RSM175-logo-white.png` | RSM 175 logo, for dark backgrounds |

You don't have to use the template. You can design your poster in any tool you like, as long as
the final poster meets the requirements below.

## Requirements

- **Size:** A0 (841 × 1189 mm), portrait.
- **Logos:** include both the RSM 175 and Imperial logos.
- **Format:** submit a single PDF, named `<groupname>-poster.pdf`, in this directory.
- **File size:** no more than 20 MB.

You may also commit your source files (e.g. `.pptx`), but `<groupname>-poster.pdf` is the version we will
mark and print.

## Friday afternoon

On Friday afternoon, staff will be visiting each group for a discussion on what you are adding to
your poster. There is no need for this to be finished when we come for the discussion, but you
should be prepared to talk about the different elements of the poster, including the solver, the
locator, and the damage mapper and risk functionality. Note these discussions will form part of
your group's poster presentation grade.

## Tips

- **Using the template:** the logos are part of the slide master, so they can't be moved or deleted
  by accident. If you want a full-page background colour or image, add it with *Format Background*
  rather than as a shape on the slide, or it will cover the logos.
- **Keeping the file size down:** dense plots (for example, maps with thousands of postcode points)
  saved as vector graphics can make the PDF very large and slow to open. Save them as PNG at around
  300 dpi, or pass `rasterized=True` to the matplotlib plotting call, before adding them to your
  poster.
- **Check your poster at full size:** zoom to 100% to make sure the text is readable and the
  figures are sharp when printed at A0.
- Use a **clear and readable font** (min 24 pt, 30 pt is better)
- Consider **colour contrast and accessibility** (e.g. colour blindness)
- **No need for a lot of text**, use bullet points and diagrams where possible
- A large image can be used to catch the eye, but make sure it is relevant to your work and not just
  decorative (images can also be used as a background, but make sure the text is still readable).
- **Only use images you have permission to use**. If you are using images from the web, check the
  copyright and licensing information, and add credit to any images you use. You can also use your
  own images or diagrams, or those created by your group.
- It is ok to be inspired by other diagrams and posters, but do not copy them directly unless you
  have permission to do so. You should acknowledge any sources you use (and you can't give other
  groups permission to copy your work!).
