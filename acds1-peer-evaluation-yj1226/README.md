# ACDS Peer Evaluation 

We hope you enjoyed the first ACDS group project. As explained in the module briefing, your mark for the project includes a mark for teamwork (weighted 10%) based on **peer evaluation**. 

Please use the process described below to evaluate the contributions of each team member to the group effort. Consider attendance and participation in team meetings and chat, individual contributions to idea generation and research, communication within the group, etc. Note that a team member can make a significant contribution even if no code written by them ends up in the final submission. Please keep in mind the overall circumstances when awarding relative contributions. These evaluations are completely confidential and will not be shown to your other team members. Please respond as honestly as you can.  

## Submission process 

### `points.csv` 

In this repository, you can find `points.csv` file. It lists your group mates, but not you. **Only change the numbers**: do not add yourself, add or remove rows, or change anyone's name. Please **modify the points column** in `points.csv` to assign a contribution score (an integer) between 0 and 40 to each team member. A greater contribution should be given a higher score. If you wish to give an equal score (20 points) to each team member, you can leave the `points.csv` file unchanged. 

Please assign points to each member of your team following these two rules: 

1. The sum of all points in `points.csv` must equal the number of people in `points.csv` times 20. For example, if there are 7 rows with people in `points.csv`, the total sum must be equal  $7 \times 20 = 140$ points. 

2. If you give to anyone 5 points or fewer, or give them more than 34 points, you **must** write a comment in the comment row at the bottom of the file (format: `Comment,"your comment in quotes"`) to justify your decision. As per the handbook, a score above 34 should imply that the team member was essential to your successful project, while a score of 5 or lower will imply that the team member made essentially no contribution to the group’s overall work. Even if you do not want to make any comments, do not remove the comment row from `points.csv`. All comments should be in this single row at the end of the file.

After you are happy with your points, please commit (and push) `points.csv` file, and **ensure that the tests in GitHub Actions workflow pass**. 

### `my-contribution.txt` 

Finally, with up to 300 words, explain what your contribution to the project was in `my-contribution.txt`. Feel free to use bullet points. If any of your contributions were performed in collaboration with another team member, please name them.
